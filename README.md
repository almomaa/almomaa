### 👋 About me

I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science & Mathematics and Program Coordinator of Data Science at **Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University**. I work at the intersection of applied mathematics, data science, and engineering, building models that are interpretable, identifiable, and robust, even when the data is noisy, sparse, or limited.

In my publications, I focus on introducing new methods, perspectives, and scientific tools. If you see a place for these methods in your own domain, I would be happy to collaborate on deploying them: I have done so in production scheduling, energy and resource allocation, weather prediction, and communication networks. This work has been supported by AFRL, NSF, ONR, and ARO, and I serve as an advisory member of [Root Dynamix](https://www.rootdynamix.com), a complex-systems solutions company.

### 🔬 What I work on

- **Causal Inference & Structure Discovery**: recovering the true drivers behind data, not just correlations (entropic regression, optimal causation entropy)
- **Explainable & Interpretable ML / AI**: geometric, entropy-based measures that make models transparent and trustworthy
- **Complex Networks & Systems**: dependency mapping, synchronization, and early-warning detection of tipping points
- **Global Optimization**: genetic algorithms, particle-swarm, and metaheuristics for hard industrial decision problems
